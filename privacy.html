1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our services, how it is used, and how it is handled and protected.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to directly provide personal information such as your real name, address, or payment details.

However, our apps may automatically collect limited data through third-party services used for advertising and analytics, such as:

  • Device information: device model, OS version, language, country/region (approx.), app version

Location data: Our standard apps do not require precise GPS location. If a specific app feature ever uses location, that app will request permission and we will update this policy accordingly.

App-specific note for Study Timer Alarm: At this time, Study Timer Alarm stores alarm settings, parent PIN, and study unlock status locally on the user's device. It does not require account registration, and it does not currently display third-party ads or offer in-app purchases.

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, including:

Our apps may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use cookies and/or advertising identifiers to deliver, measure, and personalize ads (where permitted).

You can opt out of personalized ads (or reset your advertising ID) via your Android device settings.
